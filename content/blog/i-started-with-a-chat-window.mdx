One of my uncles introduced me to using AI for coding. My first setup was Gemini chat alongside VS Code. I would move code between the two, copying it from the conversation into my project, then going back to the chat as I continued working.

I already knew the fundamentals of HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. That foundation helped me understand the code Gemini gave me. I was bringing what I had learned into a new way of building.

The back and forth was hard to work with. At the time, I wasn't familiar with editor extensions or other ways to bring AI into my development workflow. Copying between the chat and VS Code was the way I knew how to use it.

## How I work now

Today, I use Claude through both its graphical interface and the command line, and I also use Antigravity IDE.

AI is now part of my process from the beginning. I start with my own idea, brainstorm with AI, and continue working with it through to finishing the project. My use of it has grown from getting code through a chat window to involving it throughout the build.

As that workflow has changed, understanding what the AI is doing has stayed important to me. I still need to know what is being built, especially when the work moves into the backend.

## Staying involved in the backend

The areas I pay particular attention to include databases, SQL, and authentication in Supabase, along with deployment on Vercel. Those are parts of the project I want to understand as I work with AI.

Before deployment, I do end to end testing to check how the app works as a whole. That is part of my process alongside understanding the code and the backend work.

Looking back, my starting point was a conversation in one window and a project in another. I was still discovering the tools available to me and working out how to use them.

Now, AI is involved much earlier and stays with me through more of the process. My own part includes the idea, understanding what gets built, and testing the result before I deploy it. That is the workflow I am continuing to develop as I learn.
